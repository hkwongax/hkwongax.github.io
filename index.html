Autonomous Robot Car · Hardware & Embedded

Hardware,
verified in one take.

Designed, wired, and programmed an autonomous line-tracking robot car on an Arduino Nano and breadboard for the Running in HKUST track challenge. Integrated an optical bright-sensing array (front-bar trackers and a bumper sensor) with dual DC motors driven via calibrated differential PWM control.

Engineered deterministic state-machine logic to handle T/Y/+ junctions, a 360° turn, square loop, U-curve, 180° turn, S-curve, and white-wall reverse-and-stop — completing all 17 obstacle-course tasks in ~20 seconds on the first demo take.
